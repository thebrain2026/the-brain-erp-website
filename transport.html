What you get

Facilities inside Transport ERP

These modules are in the running Fleet Management ERP — desktop control room and the mobile app. Screenshots below are that app.

Vehicles

Bus, magic, van, and truck master with fuel type and status.

Drivers and helpers

Staff list, role, and who is assigned to which vehicle.

Routes and stops

Route list, add route, pickup points with time, distance, and student names where it is a school fleet.

Vehicle assign

Which vehicle runs which route, shift, trip name, and trip time.

Daily trip

Date, vehicle, route, driver, helper, trip type (morning pickup / afternoon drop / extra), start KM and end KM.

Fuel entry

Litre, rate, odometer, petrol pump, bill photo, and optional odometer photo — submitted for verification.

Fuel verification

Pending, accepted, or rejected queue so diesel is not entered without a bill.

Maintenance

Service, tyre, brake, oil, battery, repair, cleaning — amount, workshop, next service KM.

Salary and reports

Staff salary, today KM, fuel cost, accepted fuel, and role-wise reports.

Role permission

Admin, transport manager, supervisor, driver, helper, accounts, and viewer — view / create / edit / verify by module.

Mobile control

Phone app with Home, Trip, Fuel, Verify, and More. Drivers enter trip and fuel from the yard.

Dispatch board

Loaded, empty, in yard, en route — so the owner sees which vehicle is doing what today.

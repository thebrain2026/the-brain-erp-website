What you get

Facilities inside School Management ERP

These modules are in the running school software — office, student app, teacher app, and admin — not a wish list.

Fees collection

Tuition, transport, and other fee heads stay separate. Dues, fines, concessions, receipts, student ledger, and collection reports sit in one office.

Pay from the app

Guardians see payable amount, tuition versus transport, and can pay from the student app. The office sees what is collected and what is still due.

Student and parent app

Homework, assignments, notices, timetable, syllabus, attendance, exams, documents, leave, library, complaints, and chat on the phone.

Teacher app

Mark attendance, give homework, lesson plan, notice, exam, result entry, student list, parent chat, e-content, AI worksheet, timetable, and reports.

Live bus location

Parents open Transport on the phone and see whether the bus is near the stop. Office keeps routes, vehicles, and transport fees.

Complaints

A student or guardian complaint comes into the main ERP. Principal review, pending / in progress / solved, with a follow-up window.

Attendance

Student attendance, teacher attendance, absent alerts, late entry, class-wise reports, and parent notification.

Exam and report card

Exam setup, marks entry, result publish, report cards, and academic performance.

Admission and students

Enquiry, admission, student list, ID card, promotion, parent details, and class teacher assign.

Staff and payroll

Staff directory, designation, department, staff attendance, leave approval, and payroll.

Library, hostel, visitors

Books and issue-return, hostel rooms, visitor and gate records, documents, and notice approval.

AI school tools

AI help for students, AI worksheet for teachers, fee follow-up highlights, and a management dashboard for dues, attendance risk, and complaints.

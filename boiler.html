What you get

Facilities inside Boiler integration farm

The integrator gives the farmer a house boiler. This ERP ties that boiler to the farm — who has it, whether it is running, fuel, and the house — not a mill steam panel.

Integrator-issued farm boilers

Suguna-type farm boilers, and the same model of programme used by integrators such as Pakija or Six: the company issues the boiler to the contract farmer.

Given to contract farmers

Each farmer house on the integrator list has a boiler on record — not a mill drum, a farm house boiler.

Integrated into the farm ERP

Running status, fuel, and the house sit in the same software as the farm, so the integrator does not chase a second notebook.

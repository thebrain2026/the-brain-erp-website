Factory OHS

Factory OHS is for reducing factory breakdown. The software analyses the motor and the model, then tells the owner: take this model for maintenance. Every factory has a maintenance window; data is sent then so they can calculate and do the work. AI names the model so the machine does not fall and a sudden breakdown does not happen.

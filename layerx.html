What you get

Facilities inside LayerX

LayerX is layer poultry farm management software. Climate and water in the shed are automated into the same board as eggs, feed, and accounts.

Ammonia gas

Ammonia in the shed is read into LayerX, so air quality sits on the same board as birds and eggs — not only on a separate meter.

CO2

Carbon dioxide in the shed is automated into the software. The layer owner sees CO2 without walking every house with a handheld.

Water intake per bird, per day

How much water each bird drinks in a day is tracked in LayerX, shed by shed.

Nipple drinker water pressure

Nipple line pressure is automated into the software, so a weak line is visible before production drops.

Daily shed light

How much light a shed gets all day is recorded in LayerX — the full day’s light, not a guess at noon.

Shed inside temperature

Inside temperature of the shed is automated into LayerX with the rest of the house climate.

Humidity

Shed humidity comes into the software next to temperature, ammonia, and CO2.

Daily shed entry

Egg production, hen-day, broken eggs, PHY eggs, culls, mortality, feed per bird, body weight, and shed remarks every day.

Egg stock and customer schedule

Egg stock, customer schedules, taken quantity, pending quantity, and delivery status in one list.

Feed mill

Feed formula, feed production, feed issue, stock value, and shed-wise feed tracking.

Accounts

Cashbook, bank, sales, purchase, expenses, party statement, profit and loss, and export-ready reports.

Workers and health

Attendance, labour payment, vaccination, oral medicine, next-date reminders, and flock health history.

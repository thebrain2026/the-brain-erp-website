About

A different brain for each industry

Inside the ten products

What you get if you use them

LayerX

Layer poultry farm management software. Shed ammonia, CO2, per-bird water, nipple pressure, daily light, inside temperature, and humidity — automated into LayerX.

Hotel PMS

Hotel management software — the whole hotel ecosystem is automatic. Housekeeping data, restaurant orders from the guest’s mobile, owner app, manager app, and P&L by day, month, and year, hotel vs restaurant.

School management

Fees, student app, teacher app, admin, bus tracking, homework, notices, complaints, exams, and payroll — with real app screenshots.

Gym ERP

Members, batches, fees, trainers, live check-in, zone load, and this week’s renewals.

Trading ERP

Buy, sell, godown stock, party ledger, and cash — today’s khata on screen.

Transport ERP

Vehicles, routes, daily trip KM, fuel with bill photo, verification, maintenance, and the real driver phone app.

Boiler integration farm

Poultry and contract-farm boilers given to farmers and integrated — Suguna-type, not the mill-house steam boiler (that sits inside paper mill and rice mill).

Paper mill

Raw material, PM-1 GSM and reels, breaks, waste, dispatch, labour, mill accounts, and the mill-house industrial boiler.

Rice mill

Paddy purchase, milling, lot recovery, head rice, bran, husk, sales, accounts, and the mill steam industrial boiler.

Factory OHS

Reduces factory breakdown. Analyses the motor and model, then AI tells the owner which model to take into maintenance in the factory’s maintenance window.

What you get

Facilities inside Hotel PMS

Use Hotel PMS and the night manager sees the house on one screen — rooms, arrivals, housekeeping, and money still open.

Room rack

Every room on tonight’s house as occupied, vacant, or dirty, with the room number on the tile. The desk does not guess from a paper chart.

Check-in and arrivals

Tonight’s expected arrivals and who is checking in now. Guest name and room number sit on the same list.

Housekeeping

Dirty rooms stay marked until they are ready. A vacant room is not sold while it is still being cleaned.

Guest folio

Open bills and room-wise due so the cashier knows what is still unpaid before checkout — Tk on the folio, not a second notebook.

Occupancy board

In-house count versus vacant rooms, so the owner’s morning look is a number, not a walk of the corridor.

Front desk on the phone

The same rack, arrivals, and open bills can be opened on a phone during the night shift.

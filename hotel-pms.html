What you get

Facilities inside Hotel PMS

The whole hotel ecosystem is automatic. Housekeeping data, restaurant mobile orders, a separate owner app, a separate manager app, and P&L — hotel versus restaurant — all visible.

Housekeeping data

Data comes from housekeeping into the software. Dirty and ready rooms are not guessed from a paper list.

Guest and walk-in mobile ordering

Specialization is restaurant. Hotel guests order food from their own mobile. Walk-in restaurant guests order from mobile too. No paper menu card — the menu is in the app.

Owner app

A separate owner app. The owner sees the house and the money without sitting at the front desk.

Manager app

A separate manager app for the person running the floor — rooms, restaurant, and today’s house.

Profit and loss

Strongest point: profit and loss per day, per month, per year — all visible, not waiting for a month-end notebook.

Hotel vs restaurant revenue

Hotel revenue versus restaurant revenue, side by side, so the owner sees which side of the house is earning.
